About Our Ministry

For over five decades, the Altar Server Ministry at St. Louis Catholic Parish has been forming young hearts in faith, discipline, and service to God and His people.

Altar servers are young men and women who assist the priest during liturgical celebrations. Through this sacred ministry, they grow in their faith, learn reverence for the Eucharist, and develop leadership skills that serve them for a lifetime. At St. Louis Catholic Church, we take pride in our tradition of excellence and devotion.

Our History

Our Patron Saint: St. John Berchmans

In the 17th century, a young Jesuit lay brother named John Berchmans found his calling in the smallest of duties. Serving in the sacristy, maintaining the altar, and assisting during Mass, John understood a profound truth: "Faithfulness in little things is a big thing." This humble Belgian saint became the patron of altar servers, not through grand gestures, but through meticulous devotion to the sacred work of the altar.

The Berchmans Legacy in Our Parish

Our altar server ministry has been shaped by the very spirit that defined St. John Berchmans a commitment to excellence, reverence, and humble service. Like our patron saint, each generation of servers in our parish has answered the call to:

Serve with Precision

Just as Berchmans attended to every detail in preparing the altar, our servers are trained in the careful choreography of the Mass.

Foster Spiritual Growth

Berchmans saw his work as a pathway to holiness. Our servers learn that serving at the altar is not a task, but a privilege that deepens their faith.

Embody Humility

In a world that celebrates the spotlight, our servers, like Berchmans, understand that true greatness lies in faithful, unnoticed service.

From Then to Now

What began decades ago as young members answering the altar's call has evolved into a living tradition rooted in Berchmans' example. Today's altar servers continue his legacy showing up, paying attention, and serving with hearts full of devotion. Whether preparing candles, assisting the priest, or maintaining the sacred space, they echo his timeless wisdom: "In small things, faithfulness. In big things, courage. In all things, charity."

A Living Witness

Our altar servers are modern-day disciples of St. John Berchmans, proving that sanctity is found not in seeking recognition, but in serving faithfully, day after day, with reverence and love.
